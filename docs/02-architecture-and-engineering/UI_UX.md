# UI/UX

**Status:** Draft from proposal; design files remain external references

Primary experiences are the landing page, trusted onboarding, citizen dashboard, AI assistant, risk analysis, high-risk zone map, alerts, mental-wellness hub, provider portal, consultations, and printable reports.

Interfaces should use plain language, readable risk explanations, accessible color and typography, clear consent, visible limitations, low cognitive load, and an emergency escalation path. Risk colors must never be the only signal.
