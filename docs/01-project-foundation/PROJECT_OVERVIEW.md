# Project overview

**Status:** Draft — derived from `Albo_Project_Proposal.docx`

## Product

Albo is an AI-powered preventive digital health hub designed to identify health risks before severe symptoms appear. It combines consent-based environmental, behavioral, clinical, lifestyle, and wellbeing information with preventive guidance and healthcare access.

## Proposed users

- Citizens and families
- Hospitals and pharmacies
- Healthcare providers and independent consultants
- Community health workers
- Public-health or administrative users, subject to later authorization design

## Proposed capabilities

- Landing page and trusted onboarding
- Citizen dashboard with understandable health analytics
- Consent-based health and lifestyle profile
- Risk indicators for selected conditions, including malaria, cholera, respiratory illness, mental-wellness concerns, and non-communicable diseases
- Environmental and high-risk-zone maps
- Contextual notifications and preventive recommendations
- AI assistant and mental-wellness hub
- Nearby facility and consultant discovery with directions
- Provider portal, monitoring, consultations, and reports
- Printable longitudinal health analytics
- Future connected-device and electronic-health-record integrations

## Product boundary

Albo supports prevention, information, navigation, and communication. A risk indicator is not a diagnosis. High-risk, emergency, or self-harm situations require clear escalation to appropriate human or emergency services; the product must not imply that an AI response replaces clinical care.

## Intended impact

The proposal targets earlier intervention, better health awareness, improved access to care, support for resource-constrained communities, and progress toward SDG 3. Any numerical impact targets in the proposal are hypotheses until measured through a defined evaluation plan.

## Open questions

- First release geography, language, users, and clinical partners
- Conditions and risk indicators included in the first release
- Regulatory and clinical governance requirements
- Approved data sources, retention, consent, and deletion behavior
- Exact provider workflows and responsibilities
